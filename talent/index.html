Hello! I’m John. Due to a
Miracle (Piano Teaching System),
I’m fortunate to be a lifelong musician.

Over two decades of classical piano
and eight years of singing and acting
training, I’ve performed in musicals, sung
in weddings, and earned a music minor
from the University of Michigan.

Then in 2016 I veered into tech.

Since then, I am focused back on music
in blended, experiential ways. Thanks to
that tech work and my peristent geekery,
I approach the many technical sides of
events without fuss, to pull off those
special moments simply.
